01 / Vinyl Wrap

Vinyl Wrap

A full wrap changes the entire look of the vehicle while sealing the factory paint underneath. Done correctly, it comes off years later with the original finish intact. That is why cast film, proper prep and tucked edges matter more than the color you pick.

We handle full color changes, partial wraps, roofs, hoods, mirrors and accent work. Textured and specialty films are available for anyone who wants something less common than gloss.

  • Full color change and partial coverage
  • Gloss, satin, matte, metallic and textured films
  • Trim, handles and badges removed where needed
  • Paint sealed against stone chips and UV fade
  • Fully reversible with no damage to sound paint
Typical: 3–5 days Cast film Warranty included

02 / Window Tint

Window Tint

In a Georgia summer, tint is climate control as much as styling. Ceramic film blocks a large share of infrared heat and almost all UV without the signal interference of older metallic films, and it will not turn purple two summers in.

Every vehicle gets a computer-cut pattern, heat-shrunk to the curve of the glass and set with a clean edge. We will walk you through Georgia's visible light transmission rules before we cut anything.

  • Ceramic and carbon films in a range of shades
  • Serious heat and glare reduction, UV protection
  • No signal interference with phones, GPS or toll tags
  • Windshield strips and full front windshield options
  • Old film removal and adhesive clean-up
Typical: 2–4 hours Ceramic IR film GA-compliant options

03 / Chrome Delete

Chrome Delete

Factory chrome dates a vehicle faster than almost anything else. A chrome delete replaces it with gloss or satin black film. The result is sharper, more modern, and completely reversible if you ever sell the car and the next owner wants the brightwork back.

This is detail work: window surrounds, pillars, grille slats, badges, mirror caps and door handles all get wrapped individually rather than painted over.

  • Window surrounds, B-pillars and belt line trim
  • Grille surrounds, slats and lower valance accents
  • Badges, emblems and lettering
  • Mirror caps, door handles and roof rails
  • Gloss, satin or matte black, or a custom color
Typical: 1–2 days Fully reversible No paint required

Questions

Good to know

How long does a vinyl wrap last?

With quality cast film and normal care, expect roughly five to seven years on a daily driver in the Atlanta climate. Horizontal panels like the roof and hood take the most sun and usually show wear first. Garage parking and hand washing add years.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

Not on healthy factory paint. The film actually shields it. The risk is with panels that were previously repainted or already chipping, where lifting film can pull weak paint with it. We check for that before quoting and tell you straight if a panel is questionable.

What tint darkness is legal in Georgia?

Georgia sets minimum visible light transmission levels that differ for front side windows versus rear glass, with separate rules for multi-purpose vehicles. We go over the current requirements with you before cutting film so your install is road legal.

How soon can I roll the windows down after tinting?

Leave them up for three to five days while the adhesive cures. Some haze or small water pockets are normal at first and clear as the film dries. There is no need to worry unless they are still there after a couple of weeks.

Can chrome delete be undone?

Yes. The film is removed with heat and the original trim is untouched underneath. That reversibility is the main reason we use film rather than paint for this work.

How do I wash a wrapped vehicle?

Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap and a soft mitt. Skip automatic brush washes, and keep pressure washers at a distance and off the edges. Get bird droppings and fuel spills off quickly. They stain film faster than paint.
